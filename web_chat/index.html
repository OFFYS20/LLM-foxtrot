Fills in while the model writes.

Bench runs the ai_studio_transformer architecture — RMSNorm or LayerNorm, SwiGLU/GELU/SiLU/ReLU, RoPE or learned positions, grouped-query attention — on the CPU in plain JavaScript, so it suits the small models you can train at home rather than multi-billion-parameter ones. It prepends the <s> token and stops at </s>, so a continuation runs on from your text instead of starting a fresh document.
